Barcode Generator Help

Create QR codes and more than 100 other barcode formats directly in Google Sheets with bwip-js.

Quick start

Use a formula

Put the value you want to encode in A2, then enter:

=IMAGE(BWIP_QRCODE(A2))

Use the generator

Open Barcodes → Open barcode generator, choose a type, enter the contents, and select Insert IMAGE.

Create many at once

Pass a range to a barcode function. The URLs spill into the neighboring cells:

=BWIP_QRCODE(A2:A20)

Formula reference

Every named function follows the same format:

BWIP_TYPE(text, [options], [scale], [rotate])
ArgumentMeaningExample
textThe value or cell to encode.A2
optionsOptional bwip-js/BWIPP settings separated by spaces."includetext textxalign=center"
scaleA positive whole-number image scale. Default: 2.3
rotateN, R, L, or I."R"

Common examples

=IMAGE(BWIP_QRCODE(A2))
=IMAGE(BWIP_CODE128(A2, "includetext", 3))
=IMAGE(BWIP_DATAMATRIX(A2, "eclevel=200", 3))
=HYPERLINK(BWIP_PDF417(A2), "Open barcode")

Generic function

Use BWIP_URL when you know the bwip-js barcode identifier:

=BWIP_URL("azteccode", A2, "eclevel=23", 3, "N")

Popular named functions

BWIP_QRCODE, BWIP_CODE128, BWIP_CODE39, BWIP_DATAMATRIX, BWIP_PDF417, BWIP_AZTECCODE, BWIP_EAN13, BWIP_UPCA, and BWIP_GS1_128.

The sidebar contains the complete list and fills in a sample value and recommended options for each type.

Using the sidebar

  1. Select the destination cell in the spreadsheet.
  2. Open Barcodes → Open barcode generator.
  3. Choose a barcode type and enter the contents.
  4. Adjust scale, rotation, colors, or advanced BWIPP options.
  5. Choose Insert IMAGE, Insert URL, or Insert link.

The canvas preview is rendered locally in the sidebar using bwip-js. The inserted value uses an HTTPS PNG URL so Google Sheets can display it.

Troubleshooting

The cell is blank or displays #N/A

Confirm the source cell is not empty, the barcode type accepts the supplied value, and your network permits images from bwipjs-api.metafloor.com. Try opening the returned URL directly.

The sidebar says bwip-js could not be loaded

Your network or Google Workspace content policy may be blocking cdn.jsdelivr.net. Ask your administrator to allow that domain, then close and reopen the sidebar.

The barcode is too small or too large

Change the scale argument or the sidebar’s horizontal and vertical scale settings. Barcodes grow in fixed module increments, so not every exact pixel size is possible.

The scanner cannot read the barcode

Increase the scale, keep strong contrast, avoid resizing the rendered cell image, and verify that the selected barcode standard accepts the encoded characters. Printed output should retain a clear quiet zone around the symbol.

A formula is not recognized

Verify that the add-on is installed and enabled in this spreadsheet. Function names begin with BWIP_. Some spreadsheet locales use semicolons instead of commas between formula arguments.

Privacy and data handling

Do not encode secrets. Barcode contents are included in an HTTPS request URL sent to the public bwip-js image service. URLs may appear in spreadsheet history, browser history, caches, proxy logs, or service logs.
  • The add-on does not automatically read or upload the rest of your spreadsheet.
  • The selected barcode contents and rendering options are sent to bwipjs-api.metafloor.com when a PNG is requested.
  • The sidebar downloads the pinned bwip-js browser library from cdn.jsdelivr.net for local previews.
  • Your organization may block either external service through its Workspace or network policy.

Support

Include the barcode type, formula or options used, and the full error message. Do not include confidential barcode contents or private spreadsheet links.
